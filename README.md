# Volleyball resultater

**👉 [volleyball-resultater.tobias-fg.workers.dev](https://volleyball-resultater.tobias-fg.workers.dev)**

A mobile-friendly way to follow Danish volleyball. Look up schedules, results, standings and match details for every
league, star your own teams to see their next match and latest result right on the front page, and subscribe to a
group's schedule in your calendar.

All data comes live from Volleyball Danmark's own results site,
[resultater.volleyball.dk](https://resultater.volleyball.dk/tms/Turneringer-og-resultater/Soegning.aspx), so it is
always as up to date as the official pages.

Tip: add it to your phone's home screen (Safari: Share → "Add to Home Screen") and it works like an app.

This is an unofficial viewer and is not affiliated with Volleyball Danmark.
